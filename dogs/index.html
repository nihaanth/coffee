Know What You Are Buying

Full Breed vs Half Breed

Both are hypoallergenic. Both are raised the same way. The difference is lineage, predictability, and price.

Full Breed $1,150 – $1,500

Purebred Labradors & Companions

  • Both parents are registered purebreds of the same breed
  • Predictable adult size, coat colour, and temperament
  • Pedigree papers and three-generation lineage records
  • Best for shows, breeding programs, or buyers who want a known standard
Half Breed $600 – $1,100

Lab Mixes & Designer Crosses

  • One Labrador parent crossed with another hypoallergenic breed
  • Wider gene pool, which is often linked to fewer inherited conditions
  • Unique looks with the gentle, eager-to-please Lab personality
  • Best for families, first-time owners, and allergy-sensitive homes

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Are all of your dogs really hypoallergenic?

Yes. Every dog listed on this site is hypoallergenic. We also encourage allergy-sensitive families to spend time with the specific puppy before reserving, because individual sensitivity varies from person to person.

What is the difference between a full breed and a half breed?

A full breed has two registered purebred parents of the same breed and comes with pedigree papers. A half breed has one Labrador parent crossed with another hypoallergenic breed. Half breeds are priced lower, from $600 to $1,100, and full breeds run from $1,150 to $1,500.

What is included in the price?

First and second vaccinations, deworming, microchip, a full veterinary exam, a starter kit with food and a blanket carrying the litter's scent, and our two-year genetic health guarantee.

Do you ship puppies?

We do not ship cargo. We can arrange a flight nanny or ground transport within 500 miles at cost, or you are welcome to pick up in person.

Is the deposit refundable?

Yes. The $200 deposit is fully refundable up until the puppy turns 8 weeks old. After that it is transferable to a future litter.
